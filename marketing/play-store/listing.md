## Full description (4000 chars max)

Morse Translator Pro is the complete Morse code toolkit — translate text to
Morse, transmit it through your flashlight, speaker or vibration, and decode
flashing lights or beeps back into text in real time. Everything works 100%
offline, with a clean, modern Material You design.

★ TEXT → MORSE
Type anything and instantly get accurate International (ITU) Morse code, with a
clear dot-and-dash visualization. Copy or share in one tap.

★ MORSE → TEXT
Paste or type dots and dashes and read the decoded message immediately.

★ TRANSMIT YOUR MESSAGE
• Flashlight — blink your camera LED with precise Morse timing.
• Audio — generate crisp beeps with adjustable tone (400–1200 Hz) and volume.
• Vibration — feel your message as a haptic pattern.
Full Play / Pause / Stop / Repeat controls, plus Slow, Medium, Fast presets and
a custom 1–60 WPM speed slider.

★ DECODE THE WORLD AROUND YOU
• Camera decoder — point your camera at a flashing light (like another phone's
  flashlight) and watch the message appear.
• Audio decoder — detect Morse beeps through your microphone using a tuned
  tone filter that ignores background noise.
Both show live decoded text and a confidence score.

★ HISTORY & SHARING
Save your translations, mark favorites, and search your history. Share via SMS,
WhatsApp, Telegram, email or copy to the clipboard.

★ BUILT FOR EVERYONE
• Beautiful Material 3 interface with dynamic color.
• Dark mode and large-text support.
• Haptic feedback and screen-reader friendly.
• No account, no internet required, no tracking.

★ FREE + PRO
All core features are free. Upgrade to Pro (one-time purchase) for advanced
decoding, unlimited history and extra customization — with no ads, ever.

Perfect for amateur radio operators, scouts, students, preppers, teachers,
puzzle fans and anyone learning Morse code. Learn CW, send secret messages,
signal in an emergency, or just have fun.
