# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-01-01
**App:** Morse Translator Pro

## Summary

Morse Translator Pro is designed to be **private by default**. All processing
happens **on your device**. The app does not require an account, works fully
offline, and does not collect, transmit or sell personal data.

## Information we do NOT collect

- We do not collect your name, email, or contacts.
- We do not upload the text you encode/decode anywhere.
- We do not record, store or transmit camera frames or microphone audio off the
  device. Camera and microphone input is analyzed in real time, in memory, only
  while a decoder screen is active, and is then discarded.
- We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

## Information stored locally on your device

- **Translation history** you explicitly save (text, Morse, timestamp, and the
  playback settings used). This is stored in a local database on your device
  and is never transmitted. You can delete it at any time from the History
  screen.
- **App settings** (theme, speed, tone, volume, accessibility toggles).

This data stays on your device and is included only in the Android system
backup you control.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it is used |
|------------|----------------|
| Camera     | To detect flashing lights for the Camera Decoder. Frames are processed on-device and not stored. |
| Microphone | To detect Morse beeps for the Audio Decoder. Audio is processed on-device and not stored. |
| Vibrate    | To play Morse as vibration patterns. |

Permissions are requested only when you open the relevant feature and can be
revoked at any time in Android system settings.

## Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is processed by **Google Play Billing**. We do not
receive or store your payment details; these are handled by Google under their
own privacy policy.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone,
including children.

## Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be noted in the app's release
notes and in this document's effective date.
