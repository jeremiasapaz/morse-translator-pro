FAQ

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Every feature — encoding, transmitting and decoding — runs entirely on your device, offline.

Is my camera/microphone data stored?

No. Camera frames and audio are analyzed in memory in real time and never saved or uploaded.

What's in the Pro version?

Advanced decoding, unlimited history and extra customization — a one-time purchase, with no ads ever.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 12 (API 31) and newer.

Is it open source?

Yes, under the MIT license. Contributions are welcome on GitHub.
