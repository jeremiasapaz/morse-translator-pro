Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-01-01

Morse Translator Pro is designed to be private by default. All processing happens on your device. The app does not require an account, works fully offline, and does not collect, transmit or sell personal data.

Information we do not collect

  • No name, email, or contacts.
  • The text you encode or decode is never uploaded.
  • Camera frames and microphone audio are processed in memory in real time while a decoder screen is open, then discarded — never stored or sent.
  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Information stored locally on your device

Translation history you explicitly save and your app settings are stored locally on your device and never transmitted. You can delete history at any time in the app, and uninstalling removes all local data.

Permissions

  • Camera — detect flashing lights for the Camera Decoder.
  • Microphone — detect Morse beeps for the Audio Decoder.
  • Vibrate — play Morse as vibration patterns.

Permissions are requested only when you open the relevant feature and can be revoked anytime in Android settings.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store payment details.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
