Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-01-01

Morse Translator Pro is designed to be private by default. All core processing happens on your device. The app does not require an account and does not collect, transmit or sell personal data. The app displays ads via Google AdMob; see the Advertising section below for details.

Information we do not collect

  • No name, email, or contacts.
  • The text you encode or decode is never uploaded.
  • Camera frames and microphone audio are processed in memory in real time while a decoder screen is open, then discarded — never stored or sent.
  • No third-party analytics SDKs.

Information stored locally on your device

Translation history you explicitly save and your app settings are stored locally on your device and never transmitted. You can delete history at any time in the app, and uninstalling removes all local data.

Advertising

This app displays advertisements using Google AdMob. Google may use cookies and other technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this app or other websites. AdMob may collect certain information such as device identifiers, app usage, and location (general area) for ad targeting and measurement. This data collection is governed by Google's privacy policy, not ours.

You can opt out of personalized ads by adjusting your device's Google Ad settings at adssettings.google.com. Note that opting out may affect the relevance of ads you see.

Permissions

  • Camera — detect flashing lights for the Camera Decoder.
  • Microphone — detect Morse beeps for the Audio Decoder.
  • Vibrate — play Morse as vibration patterns.

Permissions are requested only when you open the relevant feature and can be revoked anytime in Android settings.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store payment details.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions? Use the contact form on the website at jeremiasapaz.github.io/morse-translator-pro/.
